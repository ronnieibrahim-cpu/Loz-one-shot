# Playing from the fifth dungeon into the sixth

A short guide for one sitting of play. The question it is meant to answer:
**does the last dungeon feel like a finale, or like a wall?** On the test
run's numbers the jump is steep — it takes about 32 quarter-hearts of damage
in the fifth dungeon and about 112 in the sixth, and two rooms (a room of
crossed shafts on the top floor, and the King himself) are a third of that.
Numbers can't say whether that steepness feels earned. You can.

## Where to start

You don't have to play the first four dungeons. Two ready-made saves, taken
from the test run's own playthrough, are in this folder:

- `save-at-d5-door.txt` — standing inside the door of the **Drowned Wood
  Shrine** (dungeon 5), with four Essences, 9 hearts, 182 Rupees.
- `save-at-d6-door.txt` — standing inside the door of the **Abyssal Keep**
  (dungeon 6), with five Essences, 11 hearts, 244 Rupees. Use this one only if
  you run out of time for the fifth.

To load one: open the game, go past the title to the three file slots, move
the cursor to an **empty** slot, press **SELECT**, and paste the whole of the
text file into the box that appears (it is long — copy all of it). Then pick
that slot as normal. `saves-loaded.png` shows where each one puts you.

The test run collects what it needs and little else, so these saves are a
little poorer than a real player's would be (fewer heart pieces and charms).
That makes them a fair worst case.

## What's between them

1. **The Shrine** (dungeon 5). Its idea: seeds you plant grow into stakes you
   can stand on, but only when the sea is low. Its item is the Reefseed. The
   boss is Rootmaw.
2. **After it**, the road to the Keep opens in three steps: the trading chain
   along the coast ends with a rod that opens a grate in the Keep; the Maku
   Tree, once you have five Essences, hands you the Master Sword and the
   clapper for the Keep's lock; and the lock is on the high cliffs north-west
   (lean into the iron at the west end of the upper ledge). The full walk is
   in the game guide, section 15, if you get lost.
3. **The Keep** (dungeon 6): three floors, six keys, three big fights (a salt
   colossus, a shadow, and the King at the top), pits and spikes. Its idea:
   what the sea drops down a shaft, a fishing line brings back up.

## What to look for

- **The first ten minutes of the Keep.** Does it tell you, without a sign,
  that this place is harder? Or do you just start losing hearts?
- **Deaths.** Note every one: which room, what hit you, and whether it felt
  like your mistake or the game's.
- **The room of crossed shafts** (top floor) and **the King**. These are
  where the test run bleeds most. Do they feel like the hardest rooms in the
  game *on purpose*?
- **Healing.** Did you find enough hearts and fairies in the Keep? Where did
  you wish there was one?
- **Getting lost.** Any room where you didn't know what the game wanted.
- **Pace.** Was there a stretch that dragged?

## What to tell me afterwards

Anything in your own words works. If it helps, these four answers are what
I need most:

1. **Finale or wall?** One word is fine, and a sentence on why.
2. **Your deaths:** room (or a description of it) and what killed you.
3. **The one room you'd change**, and what about it.
4. **Anything that looked or sounded wrong**, however small.
