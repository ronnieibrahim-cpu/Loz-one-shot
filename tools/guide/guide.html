THE LEGEND OF ZELDA · ORACLE OF TIDES

Player's Guide

Every room, every key and every boss, shown on the game's own screens. Stuck in the first dungeon? Jump straight to Tidewash Grotto.

How to use this guide: each dungeon opens with a one-line nudge. If that's all you wanted, stop reading there. Below it is the full solution, room by room. The lime green marks on the pictures are ours, not the game's: numbered circles match the numbered steps in the text, arrows show where to walk, and a dashed box shows the patch of water the Anchor holds. Tap any picture to see it bigger.

CHAPTER 1How to play

Controls

On a phone or tablet, a touch pad appears over the game: the arrow pad on the left, A and B on the right, and SEL and START in the top-right corner. On a computer, use the keyboard:

ButtonKeyboardWhat it does
Arrow pad← ↑ → ↓ or WASDWalk. Walk into a pushable block to shove it.
AX, K or SpaceTalk, read, open a chest or a locked door, pick up a pot. When there is nothing in front of you, it uses the item on A.
BZ or JUses the item on B.
STARTEnterOpens the menu: items, map, charms, quest and save.
SELECTShift or TabIn the menu, flips to the next page.
PSound on or off.

Two moves need no button at all. Walk at a one-square gap and Link hops across it by himself. And the sword charges: hold its button and let go for a spin attack.

The screen

The status bar. 1 the item on B (a new game starts with the Moon Conch here). 2 the item on A (the Wooden Sword). 3 the tide gauge: the letter says the tide right now, L, M or H. 4 rupees (and, inside a dungeon, a key and the number of Small Keys you're carrying). 5 your hearts.

You start with three hearts. Each heart takes four hits of the weakest kind, so a small enemy usually takes a quarter or half a heart and a boss's claw takes a whole one. Four Pieces of Heart make a new heart, and every boss leaves a Heart Container.

The tide: the one idea the whole game is built on

At the start, Farore the Oracle gives you the Moon Conch. Play it (it starts on B) and the sea everywhere in the world rises or falls one step. It always goes round in the same order:

LOW → MID → HIGH → back to LOW

So one press moves one step along, and two presses get you to any level from any other. When you play it a wave sweeps across the screen and the letter on the tide gauge changes.

The tide changing in the first dungeon. The wave is the new water level sweeping across the room; the gauge in the status bar already reads H.
LevelWhat the world looks like
LOWThe sea pulls back. Sandbars, reef and seafloor are dry enough to walk on. Wells in the dungeons are ankle deep. Drains become empty pits you can't cross.
MIDWhere the game starts, and where most of the world is comfortable. Shallow water can be waded; wells are too deep.
HIGHShallows become deep water. Low walls go under and can be swum over.

The rule of thumb for the whole game: if a room looks impossible, try it at the other two tides. Almost every puzzle starts there. Play the conch while you're standing on dry ground. If you raise the water while you're wading, you can end up out of your depth.

Swapping items

The items page. Move the cursor onto an item and press A to put it on A, or B to put it on B.

Press START for the menu. You only have two buttons and you'll soon own more than two items, so you'll swap often. From the first dungeon on, a good habit is: keep the conch on B, and put whichever item the room needs on A. Opening chests, reading and talking always work on A whatever item is there.

Saving, and what happens when you fall

Press START, then SELECT four times to reach the SAVE page. Choose SAVE to save and carry on, or SAVE AND QUIT to stop. The game does not save on its own, so save whenever you've done something you wouldn't want to do twice.

If you run out of hearts, you lose nothing but the hearts. Every key, item, opened door and opened chest stays yours. You wake up at the door of the dungeon you were in (or where you last came onto the screen, outside), with the tide as it was when you arrived. The game saves at that moment too.

CHAPTER 2The road to the first dungeon

JUST A NUDGE

The first dungeon is east along the coast, in the sandy screen with the stone doorway. Pick up two Pieces of Heart on the way.

After Farore's speech you're standing in Tidewatch Village with the Moon Conch and the Wooden Sword. You can walk straight to the first dungeon, but two caves on the way each hold a Piece of Heart, and the extra heart makes the first boss much easier. The route below is the one we tested, start to finish.

The coast from the village to the first dungeon. The line is where Link walked.
  1. Tidewatch Village. You start here. The shop is on the next screen east; a shield costs 30 rupees and it's worth buying as soon as you have them.
  2. West Bluff, one screen west. The cave at the top of the screen is the Bluff Grotto. Inside: a chest with 30 rupees, and a Piece of Heart sitting on the floor at the top left. (The big chest won't open until you have four Essences. Come back later.)
  3. Sunken Reef, two screens east of the village. The cave here is the Reef Hollow. The carving on its wall says "When the sea withdraws, walk where fish swam." Play the conch twice, to LOW, and walk out onto the dried seafloor for the Piece of Heart. Before you leave the screen outside, play the conch once to bring the tide back to MID, so you aren't walking over seafloor full of fish.
  4. Grotto Mouth. Keep east along the top of the sand, then down to the stone doorway set into the cliff. Walk in.
WHAT'S IN THE REEF HOLLOWThe big chest in the Reef Hollow holds the Kilnshell, a shell that makes fire. You don't need it for the first dungeon, but you will need it in the second, so you might as well open it now.

FULL SOLUTION

STOPS 1–8The south half: the conch is all you need

1 · Grotto Mouth

The first room. Stand anywhere on dry stone and play the conch twice.

The carving by the door says "The sea keeps this floor. Take it back." That's the hint for the whole south half. The room to the north is five rows of deep well from wall to wall, so play the conch twice (MID → HIGH → LOW) before you go anywhere. You'll stay at LOW all the way to the Anchor.

The Drinking Floor

At MID: deep water, no way across. At LOW: ankle deep. Walk straight north.

At low tide the wells are only ankle deep, so wade straight up the middle to the north door. Two crabs and a Keese live here. Crabs hold their claws up on the side they're facing, so hit them from above, below or behind, never head on. It's worth clearing this room: you'll pass through it again on the way out, and cleared rooms stay cleared.

The door on the west side leads to the Bone Cell, an optional room with a carving blank in it (see Extras).

2 · Sunken Hall

The hub. Each block sits one square below its floor switch.

The hub of the south half, with doors west, east and north. Beat the Zol and the crab first. Then:

  1. Stand directly below the left block and walk up into it. Keep holding up and it slides onto the floor switch.
  2. Do the same with the right block.

With both switches held down, "A light comes up out of the water.": a fairy appears in the middle of the room. Touch it to refill all your hearts. If you're already at full health, you can leave it for later.

Pushing the left block. You have to be squarely below it and keep walking into it.

3 · Map Alcove (west of the hall)

The Dungeon Map is on the floor, boxed in on three sides. Walk in from the open east side.

The map sits in the middle of the room behind three blocks, but its east side is open. Come in from the right-hand door and walk straight to it. The map shows every room, even the ones you haven't been in; press START and SELECT once to see it.

4 · Chartstone Alcove (east of the hall)

Stand just below the chest, face up and press A.

Chests open from the side you're facing. Stand just below this one, face up, and press A for the Chartstone. With the Chartstone, the map page also marks which rooms change with the tide.

Tide Gallery

The crossroads. The north door is locked. The two keys that open it and the next door are one to the west and one to the east.

This room is the crossroads of the south half, and you'll pass through it several times. The door in the north wall is your first locked door. The two Small Keys you need are in the rooms either side.

5 · Crab Pit (west of the Gallery): Small Key

Three crabs. Beat all three and a key drops in the middle.

Three shielded crabs. They scuttle side to side and turn their claws toward you when you're close, so wait for one to turn away or come at it from above or below. When the last one falls, "Something clatters onto the wet stone.": a Small Key lands in the middle of the room.

"You found a Small Key!"

6 · Switch Room (east of the Gallery): Small Key

The same puzzle as the Sunken Hall. Push each block up onto its switch.
  1. Stand below the left block and push it up onto its switch.
  2. Do the same with the right block.

"A catch releases somewhere below." A Small Key and a heart drop in the middle of the room. You now have two keys.

7 · Back to the Tide Gallery: first locked door

Walk up to the locked door, face it and press A.

Go back to the Tide Gallery and walk up to the locked door in the north wall. Face it and press A. "The lock falls away."

8 · The Locked Stair: second locked door

Two Zols, and the second locked door straight ahead.

Two Zols. Beat them, then open the locked door in the north wall with your second key. Remember this room: both halves of the north wing end in a staircase that brings you back up into the bottom-right corner here.

STOP 9The Sluicegate: the Tidewright's Anchor

The big chest, and above it the boss door. You'll be back for that door.
  1. Walk round to the top of the big chest, stand just above it, face down and press A.
"You got the Tidewright's Anchor! Throw it to hold the tide where it lands. Press again to recall it."

The plate on the wall says "Iron remembers. Sink it where you want the sea to stay." The chest sits right under the boss door, so you can see where this is all going.

HOW THE ANCHOR WORKS
  1. Put it on A. Press START, move to the Anchor and press A. That takes the sword off. Keep the conch on B.
  2. Throw it. Press A. It flies two squares in the direction you're facing and bites into the floor where it lands.
  3. It holds the tide. A square of water five squares by five around it stays at the level it was when the Anchor landed. The rest of the room still follows the conch. In the pictures, the dashed box is that square.
  4. Call it back. Press A again, from anywhere, even from another room. It comes back and the water under it goes back to normal. If the Anchor is sitting in another room, your first press only calls it back. Press a second time to throw.
If a pot is right in front of you, A picks up the pot instead. Step away from pots before you throw.

From here on, every crossing works the same way: hold one band of water with the Anchor, then play the conch for the other. A well is shallow at LOW and too deep above that. A drain is an empty pit at LOW and wadeable water at MID. So a band of wells next to a band of drains can never both be walkable at the same time... unless the Anchor holds one of them.

STOPS 10–14The east wing

Leave the Sluicegate by its east door. The tide should still be LOW.

10 · The Iron Pipe

At LOW: throw into the wells. Then MID: walk across.

Wells first (near you), drains beyond them. At LOW the wells are shallow but the drains are pits.

  1. Wade into the wells to the square two below the middle row, face up, and throw the Anchor. It bites in the middle of the wells.
  2. Play the conch once: LOW → MID. The drains fill with wadeable water. The wells around the Anchor stay shallow.
  3. Walk along the middle row, through the Anchor's patch and across the drains, to the east door.

Leave the Anchor where it is for now.

11 · The Drowned Chamber

At MID the middle is deep, with two fish in it. At LOW it's a pit and the fish are gone. Walk round the edge.

Play the conch twice (MID → HIGH → LOW). The middle drains to an empty pit and the two fish have nowhere to be. Walk up the west edge and along the top wall to the north door. Watch for the crab.

12 · The Long Race

At LOW: come up the east side and throw down. Then MID: walk west along the middle.

You come in from the south, under a row of blocks. The bands run the other way here: drains on the far (west) side, wells nearer. The door you want is in the west wall.

  1. Walk up the east side of the room, past the end of the block row, and into the wells so you're two squares above the middle row. Face down. Press A twice: the first press calls the Anchor back from the Iron Pipe, the second throws it. It bites in the wells on the middle row.
  2. Play the conch once: LOW → MID.
  3. Walk west along the middle row, through the patch and over the drains, to the west door.

13 · The Keyvault: Small Key

Stand above the chest, face down, press A.

Put the sword back on A and deal with the Keese. Then stand just above the chest, face down and press A for your third Small Key.

The third Small Key.

Back the way you came

The Anchor is still in the Long Race, so the way back is open. Out by the south door. Drowned Chamber at LOW: round the east side to the south door.
  1. Go back east into the Long Race. Don't call the Anchor back: it's still holding the wells, so at MID the whole middle row is walkable. Walk back to the east side and leave by the south door.
  2. In the Drowned Chamber, play the conch twice to LOW again, walk down the east side and leave by the south door.
  3. As you step into the next room, play the conch once: LOW → MID. The miniboss moves faster on dry ground, so fight it at MID.

14 · Clawcrab Den: the miniboss

A long room, twice the usual width. The Clawcrab waits in the middle.
MINIBOSS · CLAWCRAB

What it does. It scuttles left and right across the room. Every few seconds it freezes and flashes, then throws a fan of three rocks (half a heart each). Once it's hurt it gets angry: it lines up with you, charges the length of the room, and throws five rocks at a time.

How to win. Stay out of its row and column while it's walking; you're only in danger when you're lined up with it. When it freezes and flashes, step sideways out of the fan, then run in and hit it two or three times while it's recovering. It has no shell and no weak point: every sword hit counts. When it starts charging, move off its line the moment it stops to aim, and hit it from the side as it goes past.

At low health. Stop chasing it. Dodge until it throws, then take one safe hit after each throw and back off. There's nothing to heal you in the den, so come in with full hearts: if you're low, the fairy in the Sunken Hall refills you (see stop 2), and it's worth the walk.

Hitting it from the side. It stops and flashes before it throws. That's your cue to move.

When it falls, "The claw stops moving. The way west is quiet.", and a Piece of Heart appears where it died. Then walk to the west wall: the door there is locked, and your third key opens it.

STOPS 15–16The Two Gauges

At LOW: hold the left well empty.

Two small wells up top, and a sealed door in the middle of the room. The sign above it says "Two marks over the door: one well empty, one well full." They're too far apart for one tide to do it, so the Anchor holds one while the conch does the other.

  1. Put the Anchor on A. Play the conch twice (MID → HIGH → LOW). Both wells are now empty.
  2. Stand four squares straight below the left well, face up and throw (two presses, since the Anchor is still in the Long Race). It lands two squares below the well, so the well is inside the held patch.
  3. Play the conch twice again (LOW → MID → HIGH). The right well fills; the left stays empty. "Both marks read at once. The door gives."
At HIGH: the door opens. The Piece of Heart and the stairs are through it.

Through the door: a Piece of Heart against the wall on the left. That's your fourth piece if you took the two in the caves and the Clawcrab's, and it fills up a whole new heart. Then take the stairs in the bottom-right corner. They bring you up into the corner of the Locked Stair.

STOPS 17–19The west wing

You're back in the Locked Stair at HIGH. Put the sword on A if any Zols came back. Play the conch twice (HIGH → LOW → MID), go north into the Sluicegate, and leave by its west door.

17 · The Long Sluice: two throws

This is the crossing one Anchor can't do alone. From the east you meet drains, then wells, then drains again. You throw the Anchor, cross half the room, call it back and throw it again.

1: at MID, hold the near drains full. 2–3: LOW, walk out, throw again. 4: MID, walk out west.
  1. At MID, the near drains are water you can wade. Stand two squares below the middle row in the drains nearest the door, face up and throw (two presses). That keeps them wadeable.
  2. Play the conch twice (MID → HIGH → LOW). The wells in the middle are now shallow, and the drains you're holding are still water. Walk west along the middle row onto the wells.
  3. Stand in the wells, two squares below the middle row, face up, and press A twice: once to call the Anchor back, once to throw it into the wells. The drains behind you empty; that's fine, you're past them.
  4. Play the conch once (LOW → MID). The far drains fill, the wells around the Anchor stay shallow. Walk west out of the room.

Cistern Turn

Just passing through: out by the north door.

A Zol and a crab. Go out through the north door. (The south door leads to the optional Weeping Cistern.)

18 · The Drip Vault

At LOW: hold the top well empty. Then HIGH: the east door opens.

The same puzzle as the Two Gauges, turned on its side. The sign: "Two marks beside the door: the upper well empty, the lower full."

  1. Deal with the Keese, then put the Anchor on A. Play the conch twice to LOW.
  2. Stand in the middle of the room, four squares below the top well, face up and throw (two presses). The top well is inside the patch.
  3. Play the conch twice (LOW → MID → HIGH). The bottom well fills and the east door opens. Go through it.

19 · Bosskey Vault: the Boss Key

Stand above the chest, face down, press A. Then the stairs home.

Put the sword back on A. Stand just above the chest, face down and press A for the Boss Key. Leave the Keese alone if you like. Then take the stairs in the lower right; they bring you up into the Locked Stair again.

The Boss Key.

STOPS 20–21Boss: Gohmaraq, the Tidewash Claw

  1. In the Locked Stair, play the conch once: HIGH → LOW. Bring the tide in at LOW; the boss room keeps whatever tide you walk in with, and LOW is the one you want.
  2. Go north into the Sluicegate. The boss door is in the north wall, right above where the Anchor chest was. Face it and press A.
The boss door, straight up from the big chest. Gohmaraq. His red health bar runs along the bottom of the screen.
BOSS · GOHMARAQ

The weak point. Gohmaraq is a giant crab with a hard shell. Hitting the shell does nothing. When he slams his claw into the floor, his eye opens and stays open for a moment: that's when your sword hurts him. At LOW tide the drying shell cracks and the eye stays open twice as long, which is why you came in at LOW.

Phase 1. He scuttles side to side across the top of the room. Every few seconds he rears up, freezes and flashes, then slams, throwing a spray of three rocks (a quarter heart each). Dodge the spray, then run in and hit the open eye two or three times. Get back out before it shuts; his claw costs a whole heart.

Phase 2 (once he's lost about a third of his health). He's faster, and now he lines up and charges the length of the room. If a charge ends in a wall, he's dazed with his eye open. Stand in front of a wall, wait for the charge, step aside at the last moment and hit him while he's stunned. The slams now throw five rocks.

Phase 3 (the last third). Two crabs join in, the slams come often, and every so often he blows a ring of eight bubbles in every direction. Keep to the open floor and keep moving. Clear the crabs if they're in your way, then go back to "dodge the slam, hit the eye".

At low health. Don't get greedy. One or two hits per opening, then back off. The rocks and bubbles are small damage; the claw and the charge are the big ones, so never stand right next to him when he isn't slamming. If you fall, you start again at the dungeon entrance with all your keys and doors intact, and Gohmaraq is back at full health, so it's a long walk: take the fairy in the Sunken Hall on the way back in.

Phase 1: he patrols the top of the room. After a slam: now is the time to hit.

When he falls he leaves a Heart Container near the bottom of the room. The Essence of the Tide appears in the middle of the arena: walk onto it. "Essence of the Tide: I — the Shallow Bell." Farore tells you to visit the Maku Tree next.

The first Essence.
STUCK IN TIDEWASH GROTTO? THE THREE THINGS PEOPLE MISS
  1. The conch goes round in one direction. From MID, LOW is two presses (MID → HIGH → LOW), not one. Check the letter on the tide gauge.
  2. If the Anchor is in another room, the first press only calls it back. Press A a second time to throw. And if a pot or a sign is in front of you, A lifts or reads it instead of throwing.
  3. The Anchor holds the tide it landed in. Throw first, then change the tide. Throwing after the change holds the wrong level. In every crossing here, stand two squares short of where you want it, facing the spot, and throw.

CHAPTER 4On to the Coral Spire

JUST A NUDGE

The second dungeon is in the coral, north-east of the first. Buy a Heart at the shop on the way if you're low.

From the Grotto to the Spire. Numbers match the list below.
  1. Tidewatch Shop (Village East, the blue-roofed house). If the boss left you low, the shop sells one Heart for 10 rupees. Stand below the item, face up and press A. (You'll get "Yes" if you just keep pressing A.)
  2. Sandpiper Row, east of the Grotto Mouth. There's a crab on the path. Like all crabs, it can't be hurt from the front: stand directly above it and swing down.
  3. Spire Mouth. Go north up the east side of the map. Where the sand has one-square gaps, just walk at them and Link hops across. The Coral Spire's door is the coral archway at the top of the screen.
  4. Shell Flats (optional, but worth it). A Piece of Heart sits on a patch you can only stand on at LOW.
  5. Outer Coral (optional), right next to the Spire Mouth: another Piece of Heart, reachable at LOW or MID.

The tested route pops into the Spire first to get the fairy in its second room (so the two optional pieces are collected on full health), then comes back out for them. Go in at MID.

WHAT ABOUT THE MAKU TREE?Farore tells you to visit the Maku Tree after the first Essence. She's in Tidewatch Village, through the door at the top of the square. She'll want something of the Bell's before she can help, and that comes much later (see the Coastwise Chain), so there's no need to go yet.

4 · Anemone Cell

From the top of the stairs, go through the south door for a fairy sitting on the floor. Then go east through the Spire Concourse to the Sealed Cell.

5 · Sealed Cell: the Brineglass Lens

The big chest is in the top-left corner. Open it from above.

Stand above the big chest in the top-left corner, face down, press A: the Brineglass Lens. "Hold it up to see the room at the next tide." It's a preview only: nothing changes until you play the conch.

The Brineglass Lens.

6 · Glass Cell: Piece of Heart

You can hear wings but see nothing.

South of the Sealed Cell. The room holds the tide at LOW, and its three Keese only exist at the high tide, so they're invisible and can't be hit... unless you're holding the Lens up. Put the Lens on B for a moment (swap the conch off), hold B to see the Keese, and swing at them with A. When the last one falls, a Piece of Heart appears in the middle. Put the conch back on B.

7 · The First Fork

Take the west shaft: hop the ledge, turn the valve, walk up the flooded shaft.

North of the Sealed Cell. Two identical shafts, one each side. The sign says "Two throats, one drinks. The dry one keeps its own counsel." The west one is right.

  1. Walk up onto the shelf and hop off the west ledge.
  2. Face the valve at the foot of the shaft and press A to turn it. The conch doesn't work in here; the valve is the only thing that moves the water.
  3. The shaft fills. Walk straight up it and out of the top.

If you pick the wrong side, a staircase takes you back to the start. Nothing is lost.

8 · Reefguard Hall: the miniboss

The Reefguard waits in the west half of this long hall.
MINIBOSS · REEFGUARD

A lump of coral that walks slowly after you. Its coral plate is closed most of the time and your sword bounces off. Every couple of seconds it flashes and lifts the plate to strike, throwing three orbs (later a ring of six). The moment the plate lifts, it's open: dodge the orbs and hit it. At HIGH tide the plate stays up much longer (130 frames against 80), so raising the tide makes this fight easier. Once it's hurt it also charges in straight lines; step aside and hit it as it stops.

Hit it while the plate is up.

It drops the second Small Key in the west half of the room. Kill the urchin in the east half too, then go through the south door at the east end.

Open this chest from the side.

Bombs.

MINIBOSS · TIDESHADE

Nereth's shadow. It chases you (three quarters of a heart a touch) and casts ink. When it's hurt it thins out, reappears and fires rings of ink. Fight at LOW (six shots per ring instead of eight, and you're not swimming). Don't trade touches: after each hit, back right off before it catches you again.

BOSS OF THE ISLAND · BRINEHULK

A salt colossus. Brine dissolves salt: at LOW it's armoured and can't be hurt at all, and you arrive at LOW. FINAL BOSS · NERETH

The trick. Nereth holds the sea in place. In each of his first three phases he pins the tide to one level, and while the tide is at that level he's sealed and can't be hurt. Play the conch once, to any other level, and he's open for about a second; he'll flash and drag the tide back after a few seconds. He's also open for a moment just after each of his attacks.

Phase 1 (pinned at MID). He walks after you and throws three tridents. Press the conch, then hit him.

Phase 2 (pinned at HIGH). Faster, rings of ten bubbles, and Wizzrobes. Same answer: break the pin, hit him.

Phase 3 (pinned at LOW). He sweeps the room with a spiral of beams and calls Stalfos. Keep moving with the spiral.

Phase 4. The pin breaks, a Darknut appears (ring the Rod to open its armour, or just avoid it), and every few seconds he turns the tide himself and is left wide open. That's your moment every time. Between openings he throws five tridents and rings of twelve bubbles, and calls Keese.

At low health. Don't get pinned against a wall by his summons: walk out through them rather than dodging into the bricks. Go in at MID with a full bar from the Stairhead fairy.

Break the pin, then strike. Later phases fill the room.

When he falls, the sixth Essence appears on the right side of the throne room. Walk into it. The Tide Bell is whole.

Charms

Charms are carved bone that change how you play. Open them on the CHARM page of the menu (START, then SELECT twice). You have three cases, one for each tide, and a charm only works while the water under your feet is at its case's level: a charm in the LOW case sleeps at HIGH. You start with the MID case; the LOW case opens at 2 Essences, the HIGH case at 4, and at 6 every case holds two. A few charms fit any case.

The scrimshander in Tidewatch Village (by the noticeboard on the east side of the square) carves charms for you: bring her a blank (a piece of bone or shell, found in some rooms and dredged from the seafloor) and some rupees, then come back after the tide has turned all the way round.

Stuck anywhere?

THE THREE THINGS PEOPLE MISS, ALL GAME LONG
  1. Try the room at all three tides. Play the conch while standing on dry ground and look at what moved.
  2. A button can only hold one item. If a verb "does nothing", check what's on A and B (START). Opening chests and doors always works on A.
  3. Crabs are hit from above or below, Darknuts after the Rod rings, and Barnacles never. If a fight isn't working, it's usually the angle, not the damage.
